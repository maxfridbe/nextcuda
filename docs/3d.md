# 3D

`model3d.generate` (a mesh from a picture, or - given a `mesh` or a `file` made here - that mesh painted like the
picture) and `model3d.rig`. The models are in [models.md](models.md): TRELLIS.2, Hunyuan3D-2mini and 2.1, UniRig, RigNet,
AnimateAnyMesh; settings in [environment.md](environment.md) (`NS_M3D_*`).

## Hunyuan3D 2.1 paint on its own

`hunyuan3d-2.1-paint` is 2.1's paint model without its shape model: the 2.5D multiview UNet draws six views of albedo and
metallic-roughness (15 UniPC steps) that are baked into a UV atlas. Give `model3d.generate` a mesh (from any engine) and
the picture it should look like - `nextcuda model3d gen PICTURE --mesh FILE.glb` - and it comes back painted. The full
`hunyuan3d-2.1` paints the shape it makes the same way.

- **On a 12 GB card** (2026-10-21): the weights are 5.9 GiB, the scratch arenas are sized from the card's free memory
  (`NS_M3D_HY_PAINT_KEEP_MIB`, `_TMP_MIB`; at least the measured peaks) and DINOv2-giant and the reference UNet are
  loaded for a run's conditioning and let go before its 15-step loop - the one model here that moves its weights
  (`NS_M3D_HY_PAINT_TRACE=1` prints the card's free memory at each phase). A 40k-face fox: the 5070 83.9 s at 11.4 of
  12.2 GiB, the 3060 190.6 s at 11.8 of 12.3 GiB - nothing else may be on the card.
- It is a different model from the Hunyuan3D-2 paint (mmgp) the platform used before: PBR output, no GGUF either way.
